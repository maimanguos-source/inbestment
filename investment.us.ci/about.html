1. Our Founding Story

Founded in 2024 by Marcus Sterling, CFA, Capital Growth Journal was created in response to the epidemic of speculative "fin-fluencer" content across social media. Too many young investors were being lured into zero-sum day trading, volatile meme coins, and predatory option contracts that inevitably destroyed hard-earned savings.

We believe that real wealth creation is boring, systematic, and rooted in empirical evidence: owning broad baskets of profitable global enterprises, capturing dividends, minimizing management fees, and letting the mathematics of compound interest compound over decades.

3. Our Editorial Standards & Fact-Checking Process

Every guide, comparison table, and financial ratio published on Capital Growth Journal undergoes a rigorous 3-tier editorial review:

  • Primary Source Verification: All financial data (expense ratios, historical returns, dividend yields) is pulled directly from audited SEC EDGAR filings (10-K, 10-Q), official fund prospectuses (Vanguard, Schwab, BlackRock), and Federal Reserve FRED databases.
  • CFA Peer Review: Every article is reviewed by a qualified investment professional to ensure analytical accuracy and adherence to CFA Institute Code of Ethics.
  • Zero Paid Endorsements: We never accept sponsored articles or compensation to artificially inflate fund rankings. If a fund appears in our top 5 index funds list, it earned that placement strictly based on rock-bottom expense ratios, liquidity, and tracking error accuracy.
